# Mechanical prototype – working assumptions

These are **initial design ideas**, not validated dimensions.

| Item | Initial idea |
|---|---|
| Coordinate system | X from luff to leech; Y from tack to head; Z away from sail |
| Sail module | 80 × 50 × 20 mm, 3D-printed shell |
| Clamping | Removable plate with two screws along clamped beam length |
| Beam | Approx. 100 mm total X length, with 30 mm clamped and 70 mm exposed |
| Beam cross-section | To measure Y bending: make beam thin in Y and broader in Z, e.g. 2 mm Y × 10 mm Z candidate |
| Gauge | One longitudinal strain gauge on the X–Z face near clamp exit |
| Beam material | Plastic or fibreglass candidate, to be tested |
| Silicone tail | Removable, tapering sleeve, possibly beginning beyond the gauge |
| Mounting options | Removable straps/Velcro, adhesives, sewn pockets or magnets; not yet chosen |

Open questions: whether stiffness is low enough for weak airflow, how tail mass changes gravity loading, whether the sleeve slips, and how to protect fine wires. Initial tests are dry and use external electronics. Waterproof module, rechargeable power, wireless links and final sail mounting are future stages.
